## 👤 About me

I work at the intersection of **psychology and cybersecurity**. I completed a cybersecurity internship at **SeAMK (Seinäjoki University of Applied Sciences)**, where I contributed to two EU-funded projects, and I'm completing a Bachelor of Business Administration in Business Information Technology (Cybersecurity) at **Laurea University of Applied Sciences**.

Before moving to Finland in 2021, I trained as a psychologist in Brazil, with postgraduate studies in psychoanalysis and neurology/neurosurgery. That background shapes how I approach security: most incidents involve people, so good security has to work for people.
